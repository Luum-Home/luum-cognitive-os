## Decision
Drive the dormant+aspirational ratio below 40% via a structured exercise pipeline: classify all agentic primitives into 4 risk tiers (A-Safety-Critical, B-Infrastructure, C-Advisory/Feature-Gated, D-Skills+Rules Metadata) using a waterfall regex classifier (`scripts/cos_classify_coverage.py`), then generate chaos-test skeletons (`scripts/cos_chaos_template.py`) and exercise them wave by wave, starting with Tier A.

## Why
As of 2026-04-20, classification counts were REAL 183 / DORMANT 580 / ASPIRATIONAL 126 / METADATA 68 (total 957), giving a 79.4% dormant+aspirational ratio. An unexercised primitive means silent failures in blockers could let destructive operations, secret leaks, or undetected regressions pass through the system unnoticed. Manual audit of 706 dormant primitives was judged too slow; bash hooks cannot be instrumented by `coverage.py`, so process-level chaos tests (subprocess invocation) were the only viable systematic approach.

## Consequences
- Positive: safety-critical hooks get verified before production incidents happen; the ratio becomes a trackable, actionable metric; new primitives ship with chaos-test skeletons as a force multiplier.
- Negative: ~5-10 minutes overhead per Tier A primitive to complete a skeleton; some Tier A primitives need complex environment mocking (skipif accepted); classifier heuristics will misclassify edge cases, requiring manual `TIER_OVERRIDE` comments.
- Reaching <40% requires exercising ~250+ currently-dormant primitives across all waves — explicitly an ongoing initiative, not a single sprint.
