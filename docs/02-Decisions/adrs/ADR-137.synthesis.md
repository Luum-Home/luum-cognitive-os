## Decision
Commit Cognitive OS to a directional trajectory from "Framing B" (governance layer wrapped around an existing harness/agent) to "Framing A" (COS as a runtime that travels with the agent — boots via `cos-init`, registers with a central Engram, dispatches through configured providers, returns evidence via the ADR-136 runway). Framing B remains fully operational during the transition with no deprecation. Every new flow under the bootstrap plan must carry an explicit framing-exercise statement (how much of Framing A it exercises) and cannot promote beyond `lab` tier without one; no flow primitive may reach `core`/`team` tier until at least one flow has shipped end-to-end in Framing A.

## Why
Framing B is operationally sound for the maintainer's day-to-day workflow and for absorbing new harness capabilities as they ship, but it is architecturally incompatible with the cloud-worker maintenance-flow use case the bootstrap plan targets — a wrapper that lives only on the maintainer's machine cannot fulfill an agent that must act as a COS-native runtime elsewhere. This ADR names the destination so the bootstrap plan's flows have a directional anchor to be evaluated against, rather than each flow being justified case-by-case with no shared trajectory.

## Consequences
Positive: gives the bootstrap plan a concrete evaluation lens; converts ADR-064 (harness-agnostic COS) from an aspirational, separately-paced roadmap item into something flow-gated — its completion is paid for by the flows that need it; the ADR-136 runway primitives (cross-instance bundle/import/lock/drill) gain a second purpose as runtime transport, justifying their continued existence beyond drills. Negative/risk: naming a trajectory creates pressure to show progress, which the acceptance criteria try to convert into observable signal (a counter, not a vibe); risk of Framing B being misread as "going away" when the ADR explicitly preserves it; if no flow ever needs full ADR-064 coverage, ADR-064 stays permanently partial, which the ADR accepts as a valid (if uncomfortable) outcome.

## Status & current state
Accepted for the trajectory itself (firm directional commitment); `implementation_status: planned` — individual priority shifts are explicitly NOT scheduled by this ADR and only become required artifacts when a specific flow needs them. Acceptance is measured by: (1) the next bootstrap-plan flow carrying a framing-exercise statement in skill metadata, (2) ADR-064's gaps reflected as backlog with owners in `bootstrap-portability.md`, (3) a `framing_a_flows_active` counter in `manifests/federation-triggers.yaml` — zero after three flows signals the trajectory is being talked about, not lived, (4) no flow primitive promoted to `core`/`team` before a Framing-A end-to-end flow ships. This is explicitly named as an orthogonal axis to ADR-132's Shape A/B (maintainer topology) — the near-term committed configuration is `(Shape A, Framing A)`.
