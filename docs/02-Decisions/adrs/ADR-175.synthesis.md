## Decision
Add a lightweight, non-blocking research-quality scorer running as a PostToolUse Edit/Write hook (`hooks/research-quality-validator.sh`) on `**/docs/06-Daily/reports/*.md`. It scores every report on four weighted dimensions — symmetric citation (40%, every comparison row must cite file:line on both sides), confidence levels (25%, HIGH/MEDIUM/LOW per claim block), numerical specificity (20%, numbers backed by captured commands), falsifiable claims (15%, explicit Uncertainties/Trust Report section) — logs to `.cognitive-os/metrics/research-quality.jsonl`, and emits a stderr WARNING (never blocks) when score < 70. A streak advisory fires via `consequence-evaluator.sh` when the last 5 reports average below 70. Also extends `lib/dispatch_model_advisor.py` with the ADR-069 four-dimension risk score (AC clarity, blast radius, reversibility, decision count); tasks scoring ≥5 get an automatic `opus` routing recommendation logged to `.cognitive-os/metrics/model-recommendations.jsonl`.

## Why
The codebase had no enforcement of research quality specifically — the existing consequence-system tracked trust-score streaks (agent self-reporting) but had no dimension measuring the artifact itself (symmetric citation, confidence, numerical specificity, falsifiability). The 2026-05-05 asymmetric-depth incident above is the concrete motivating failure this closes.

## Consequences
Positive: the 2026-05-05 asymmetric-depth bug becomes machine-detectable; risky tasks (irreversible, wide blast radius, vague AC) get auto-routed to opus instead of silently going to sonnet; scoring is pure stdlib regex, zero external dependencies, zero per-report cost (ADR-049 cost discipline). Negative: the asymmetric-depth heuristic is regex-based and may miss cases where evidence is semantically present but not syntactically `file:line`; the threshold of 70 is a convention, not calibrated against historical data (though env-overridable); the numeric-density heuristic (5 numbers per fenced block) may penalize reports that intentionally use bullet-style numeric summaries.

## Status & current state
Accepted and implemented (2026-05-05). Both `SO_KILLSWITCH=1` and `DISABLE_HOOK_RESEARCH_QUALITY_VALIDATOR=1` bypass the hook entirely. Latency hard-capped at 1s via `timeout 1` (target <300ms). Verification: `tests/unit/test_research_quality_advisor.py`, `tests/unit/test_research_quality_validator_hook.py`, `tests/unit/test_dispatch_model_advisor_adr069.py`. Rejected alternatives: hard-blocking below threshold (would discourage exploratory drafts), LLM-based scoring (cost would dominate the loop), a new top-level package (extending consequence-system keeps the streak surface unified), reusing trust-score streak alone (different signal — agent self-report vs. artifact quality).
