## Decision
Operationally activate the skill lifecycle promotion ladder (doctrinally declared in ADR-177) with five concrete artifacts: `scripts/cos-promotion-proposer` (evaluates sandbox skills against SkillStore thresholds: record_count>=50, success_rate>=0.85, judge_avg>=0.8), `scripts/cos-demotion-proposer` (flags advisory/blocking skills with zero records in a 90-day window), a doctrine-proposer metrics upgrade (emits to `doctrine-proposals.jsonl` with input_signals from skillstore/dogfood/drift/aspirational), a weekly SessionStart hook (`hooks/promotion-proposer-weekly.sh`, throttled to once per 7 days, fail-open), and a propose-only invariant contract test that hashes `primitive-lifecycle.yaml` and the registry lock before/after running both proposers.

## Why
ADR-177 made the doctrinal decision to wake the promotion ladder but left three operational gaps unresolved: (1) 18 sandbox skills on disk had no scheduled evaluator against SkillStore evidence, so none could ever promote; (2) advisory/blocking skills with zero recent invocations had no scheduled demotion check; (3) the doctrine-proposer wrote no evidence trail of when proposals were generated or from what input signals. ADR-180 is intentionally narrow — it does not change ladder thresholds or `lifecycle_state` meanings, only adds a propose-only mechanism operators can run, audit, and approve.

## Consequences
- Positive: operators get a dated, reviewable evidence trail of every promotion/demotion proposal; metrics emission enables future SLO tracking (proposal-to-decision latency, acceptance rate); the propose-only invariant test makes auto-apply regression unambiguous; the weekly hook makes the ladder actually active without blocking sessions.
- Negative: threshold values (50 records, 0.85 success, 0.8 judge avg, 90-day demotion window) are conventional and not yet validated against COS-specific history — first 90 days will calibrate; the 90-day demotion window may over-demote rare-but-important skills (e.g. release-os, audit-integrity) with no per-skill grace allow-list yet; the metrics file grows monotonically with rotation left as follow-up.

## Status & current state
Accepted, implementation_status: implemented. Falsifiable claim set for 90-day check: at least 5 sandbox skills should promote from real evidence (0 promotions despite 18+ sandbox skills would indicate a broken mechanism), and `dogfood_score.skill_coverage` should rise from 24.07 toward 60 within 6 months. Verified via unit/integration/contract test lanes plus the propose-only SHA invariant guard on 2026-05-05.
