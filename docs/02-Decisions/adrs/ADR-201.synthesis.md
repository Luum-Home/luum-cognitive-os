## Decision
Introduce a Maintainer Agent as a bounded, scheduled, propose-only runtime role that owns the loop: observe telemetry -> detect drift -> propose change -> validate candidate -> request approval -> measure post-change impact. It may generate proposals, branches, candidate skill drafts, and ADR amendments, but may not auto-merge, auto-promote core/team primitives, or silently mutate live runtime surfaces. A first-class `PromoteFromTelemetry` primitive converts repeated runtime evidence into typed improvement proposals, gated by a canonical performance ledger that must validate signal quality (valid/suspect/corrupt classification) before any rollup can drive a proposal.

## Why
The data existed in fragments (skill invocation/feedback, router/dispatch fallback, trust-report outcomes, cost/rate-limit events, action receipts, primitive readiness ledgers) but disparity never became action unless a human happened to notice scattered JSONL signals. The performance ledger is a hard dependency and explicit blocker: `PromoteFromTelemetry`, the maintainer runner, and impact scoring must not ship as production behavior until the ledger can compile a validated rollup from fixture and live telemetry, with malformed rows (e.g. a trust score defaulting to 75 with no source, or a skill-feedback row containing a person's name instead of a skill id) quarantined rather than allowed to drive promotion/demotion decisions.

## Consequences
Positive: self-improvement stops depending on a human noticing scattered signals; degraded skills/routers/providers can produce a reviewed fix proposal before users experience repeated failures; dormant primitives can be decommissioned on evidence rather than memory. Negative: adds a new background role that must itself be bounded and observable; proposal quality depends entirely on source telemetry quality; a noisy maintainer agent becomes operational debt unless cooldowns, deterministic proposal IDs, and duplicate suppression hold; SQLite introduces a local schema/migration surface. The maintainer agent is explicitly barred from merging to main, bypassing ADR/proposal gates, mutating secrets, promoting to core/team without human approval, or lowering safety gates to improve apparent success rate.

## Status & current state
Accepted and implemented through Phase 5 impact measurement. The SQLite performance ledger, signal-quality quarantine, `PromoteFromTelemetry`, `cos-promote-from-telemetry`, and `cos-maintainer-agent --once --dry-run` are present and tested; `cos-maintainer-impact` measures whether ledger rollups actually changed operator decisions, with recorded dogfood adoption entries (DX Tax decisions, governance policy adoption, telemetry-adoption decisions) as first real evidence. Scheduled automation and mutation remain future/opt-in — the maintainer agent has not been given autonomous scheduling or write authority.
