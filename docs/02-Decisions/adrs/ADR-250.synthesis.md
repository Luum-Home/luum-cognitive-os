## Decision
Split the skill router into an explicit pipeline: skill catalog → candidate retriever adapter → COS policy/intent guard → telemetry/feedback ledger → suggest/block/no-op. Hard rules: `lib/skill_router.py` may keep the zero-dependency `regex_frontmatter` adapter as the default hot-path implementation; community retrieval stacks must live behind declared adapters and must not be imported directly by the router; COS policy guards (dangerous-skill rejection, negative context, profile/harness scope, bypass rules, telemetry) remain first-party, never delegated; any new adapter requires manifest declaration, license/footprint classification, benchmark fixtures, and ADR-249-style behavioral proof; router confidence changes require measurement, not intuition. New surfaces: `manifests/skill-router-retrieval.yaml`, `scripts/skill-router-retrieval-audit.py`, `scripts/skill-router-benchmark.py`.

## Why
External prior art (OpenAI Agents SDK deferred Tool Search, LlamaIndex router/retriever patterns, Haystack/LangChain retriever abstractions) confirmed this is a well-understood problem space that COS risked reinventing badly rather than adopting selectively — while still needing to keep governance-specific policy (dangerous-skill rejection, scope, bypass rules) first-party rather than delegated to generic retrieval frameworks.

## Consequences
Positive: prevents `skill_router.py` from becoming an unbounded custom retrieval stack; enables community adoption without bloating hook-fast-path dependencies; creates a measurable path from regex routing to BM25/ToolSearch/provider-native retrieval; separates governance policy from candidate retrieval. Negative: adds another manifest/audit surface; static import audits cannot prove semantic quality (benchmark fixtures and telemetry are still required); community adapters remain candidates until footprint and benchmark evidence justify adoption.

## Status & current state
Accepted — Slice A implemented. Adapter posture: `regex_frontmatter` is default/active; `bm25_local` and `provider_toolsearch` are candidates; `llamaindex_router`, `haystack_retriever`, `langchain_retriever` are lab-only (too heavy for default hook-path). Benchmark fixtures cover known false-positive routing risks and one known gap: "create a new skill" currently routes to `/sdd-new` instead of `/add-skill` — declared as an explicit warning, not hidden.
