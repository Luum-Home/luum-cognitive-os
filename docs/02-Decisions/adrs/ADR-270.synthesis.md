## Decision
Introduce eight primitives that mechanize the evidence-gathering and bookkeeping parts of legal compliance while leaving all counsel judgment manual: `cos-uspto-patent-search` and `cos-uspto-trademark-search` (query USPTO PatentsView/TSDR, classify CRITICAL/HIGH/LOW relevance or LIVE/DEAD/pending), `cos-counsel-packet` (bundles Annex F + USPTO reports + license snapshot + clean-room evidence into a zip), `cos-counsel-outreach-draft` (template email drafts only, no send), `cos-legal-approve` (atomically flips Annex F `reviewed-by-legal: yes` only when a counsel memo file exists, records SHA-256 in the ledger), `cos-adoption-unfreeze` (per-tool unfreeze gated on ledger + USPTO pre-flight checks), an append-only `manifests/legal-review-ledger.yaml`, and `hooks/legal-review-required-on-runtime-import.sh` (blocks commits importing attribution-tagged code from a tool not yet ledger-approved).

## Why
Counsel judgment cannot be automated, but packet assembly can — the goal was to shrink operator time per adoption from roughly half a day of manual evidence-gathering to about 30 minutes of counsel-review prep, and to make the 30-tool silent-debt backlog mechanically tractable one `cos-counsel-packet` at a time. The runtime-import hook closes a specific inverse loophole: an engineer landing attributed code before the legal pipeline completes.

## Consequences
- Positive: every approval carries a memo SHA-256 anchor + ledger entry + Annex F frontmatter mutation as an audit trail; operator time per adoption shrinks substantially; the runtime-import hook prevents attribution-bearing code from landing pre-approval.
- Negative: six new CLIs to maintain with USPTO API schema-drift risk; the ledger becomes a privileged, append-only-enforced write target (`tests/contracts/test_legal_review_ledger_append_only.py`); `cos-legal-approve` mutates Annex F frontmatter directly, reversible only by ledger-amendment commit plus manual edit; memo SHA-256 anchors evidence existence, not legal sufficiency.

## Status & current state
Accepted (2026-05-11), implementation_status: implemented, verification level "strong". All eight primitives and the manifest/hook are listed in `implementation_files`. Verified via `python3 -m pytest tests/unit/test_cos_legal_approve.py tests/contracts/test_legal_review_ledger_append_only.py -q`. Email sending remains intentionally out of scope (drafts only); global freeze flip-to-false remains a separate operator decision.
