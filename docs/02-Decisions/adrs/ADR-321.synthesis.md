## Decision
Classify every primitive along four orthogonal axes instead of scope alone: (1) **Scope** — `os-only`/`project`/`both` (install surface); (2) **Plane** — `control-plane`/`user-plane`/`factory-plane`/`runtime-plane` (why it exists); (3) **Consumer surface** — `projected`/`shared`/`maintainer-only`/`project-generated`; (4) **Proof level** — `none`/`batch`/`family`/`primitive-specific` (evidence strength). `SCOPE` must never be used as a proxy for `plane`. `both` requires proof level at least `family` for high confidence. Expected scope-distribution ratios per primitive family (hooks, skills, scripts, templates) are monitored as review signals, not hard failures. Audit surfaces (`primitive-scope-balance-audit`, `-plane-audit`, `-generic-os-only-audit`, `-false-both-audit`, `-health`, `-proof-audit`) enforce this; CI escalated from warning mode to strict ratchets after the first health-dashboard queue was triaged on 2026-05-15. `review_exemptions` are allowed only as small, explicit, path-specific, budget-capped suppressions — never a hidden allowlist — and become stale (must be removed) once the underlying detector signal stops firing.

## Why
A pure `SCOPE` taxonomy conflated "where a primitive can be installed" with "why it exists," so a high `os-only` count looked suspicious even when those rows were legitimately control-plane/factory-plane machinery needed to build and govern the OS itself. Two concrete false signals during the 2026-05-15 ratchet promotion: a case-insensitive path detector falsely flagged lowercase route examples like `internal/users/` as source-checkout evidence (fixed by making it case-sensitive to real `/Users/...` paths); and `pattern-audit` was a true over-internalization finding, reclassified to `both` because its grep/regex protocol is repo-agnostic. Five generic-looking COS status/audit skills needed exemptions until the detector learned to treat broad control-plane markers as internal evidence, after which the exemption budget dropped to zero.

## Consequences
A high `os-only` count is acceptable only when explainable by plane and evidence, not raw counts. New primitives must declare/derive scope, plane, consumer surface, and proof level before being considered high-confidence. Scope health is judged by evidence + plane + expected family distribution. The skills `project_min_warning` threshold was moved from 5 to 4.5 to stop treating a 4.9% ratio as a false rounding-edge bug signal.

## Status & current state
Accepted and implemented; supersedes ADR-314. Proof-level budgets have been driven to zero across all scopes in three closure passes: (1) `both`/`project` budgets zeroed with a 44-primitive project-scope family proof, (2) `os-only` budget zeroed via an os-only family proof, (3) final proof-debt pass added `tests/red_team/portability/test_os_only_scope_family.py` plus behavior-evidence rows for every remaining `os-only proof_level:none` primitive, and corrected six stale `lifecycle-declared-consumer-candidate` rows to `lifecycle-declared-maintainer`. Current standing budget: `none_by_scope: {both: 0, project: 0, os-only: 0}`.
