## What it is
The H1 pre-public-readiness audit: a reconciliation of commercial-facing documentation (`executive-summary.md`, `features.md`, `value-proposition.md`, `master-plan-checklist.md`, `README.md`) against the actual REAL/DORMANT/ASPIRATIONAL status of OS components, closed and signed off on 2026-05-08.

## Key mechanics
- Methodology: ground truth pulled from `rules/RULES-COMPACT.md` status annotations (`MAPE-K(inactive)`, `singularity (inactive)`, `agent-communication Valkey(OFF)`), a fresh `scripts/aspirational_audit.py` re-run (1019 components: 317 REAL, 182 DORMANT, 36 ASPIRATIONAL, 61 METADATA, 21.4% D+A ratio), verified `.claude/settings.json` hook wiring for the "14-layer safety mesh" claim, and verified existence of `lib/cost_predictor.py`/`scripts/cost_predict.py`. Claims were re-labelled, never deleted.
- Ground-truth capability table covers 16 capabilities; most are REAL (Engram, SDD 10-phase, quality-governance hooks, cost prediction, shadow-git replay, sandbox tiers, detached daemon, MCP surface, industry presets, multi-IDE portability, FSL-1.1-MIT licensing, the "300x acceleration" case-study claim). Three are DORMANT and were the actual re-label targets: Self-improvement loop (propose-only per ADR-201, autonomous mutation gated by ADR-201/204/206), SRE/self-healing MAPE-K (inactive per RULES-COMPACT, `lib/singularity.py` exists but inactive), and "Automation workflows" ticket-to-prod (pipeline templates exist but no turnkey end-to-end orchestration ships pre-wired). The 14-layer safety mesh is REAL-with-caveat: 11/14 layers wired as hooks, 3 conditional/library-call.
- Per-file changes: `features.md` gained a Status legend and per-row Status column (REAL=15, DORMANT=4 in the public matrix), with rows 4/15/17 re-labelled DORMANT and the "AI fixes problems while you sleep" claim replaced with "advisory monitoring + remediation registry with governed (human-approved) execution." `executive-summary.md` gained a Status column and its closing tagline was rewritten from "autonomous engineering teams" to a propose-only/human-gated framing. `value-proposition.md`'s DIY-stack comparison table reworded the self-improvement row. `README.md` gained a Feature status legend paragraph and a footnote on the 14-layer safety-mesh claim. `master-plan-checklist.md` received **no edit** — the orchestrator-claim-gate hook flagged unrelated line-shift side effects, so the audit treats it as an internal execution tracker out of scope for H1 rather than modifying it.
- Five open questions were raised and resolved in a 2026-05-08 operator sign-off: (1) no date-stamp added to the multi-agent-orchestration claim for v1.0; (2) `features.md` row-15/§10 heading-numbering drift deferred to a post-launch doc-cleanup pass; (3) the private `01-commercial-brief-v2` strategy-file reference stays private by design, no sanitized public version planned; (4) the 14-layer safety-mesh footnote was added in this commit; (5) the 21.4% DORMANT+ASPIRATIONAL ratio is informational only, no public claim depends on it.
